To make the dashboard your own, replace everything in this folder. Nothing
outside it defines a colour, a typeface or a logo.

  1. logo.svg, logo-reversed.svg, favicon.svg: your own marks.
  2. colors.css: your values. Keep the token names.
  3. typography.css: your font import and two families.
  4. brand.js: your name and link.
  5. Delete this notice.
