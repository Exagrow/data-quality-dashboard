## 1. Data: `data/`

Summaries and a few example records of trip data published by the New York City
Taxi and Limousine Commission (TLC). It is the city's data, not Exagrow's, and
Exagrow adds no conditions to it. What Exagrow added in producing these files
(the summaries, the rule results, the choice of examples) is under the MIT
License in section 4.

New York City issues no licence for it. Its Open Data Law (Administrative Code
section 23-502) makes public data available "without any registration
requirement, license requirement or restrictions on their use", and the city
gives no warranty of its accuracy. If you publish it, name the source: NYC Taxi
and Limousine Commission trip record data.

- Source: https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page
- The law: https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin/0-0-0-216807
